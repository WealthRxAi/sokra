Honest questions
Why is the plan free?

Because the person holding a bill they can't pay shouldn't pay to find out their options. Bill negotiators take 18–25% of what they save you. We'd rather give you the words and let you keep the money.

Then why charge for documents?

Writing a letter that cites the right program, quotes the right amounts, and reads like it came from someone who knows the rules takes real work — and real API cost on our side. $29 flat covers it and keeps Sokra running without ads or selling your data.

Will Sokra negotiate for me?

No. Sokra never contacts your biller. You do — with the exact words. That's on purpose: when you make the call, you learn how, and the next bill you won't need anyone.

Is this legal advice?

No. Sokra is financial education and advocacy scripting. For a lawsuit, garnishment, or eviction, talk to legal aid — the plan will point you there.

What about clinics, credit unions, counselors?

If you help people with bills for a living, write to sokra@llcreativityllc.com. There's a version of Sokra built for the counselor in the room.
