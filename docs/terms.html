What Sokra is

Sokra is a financial education and advocacy tool. It reads a bill, points out possible errors, lists programs and negotiation approaches that commonly apply, and gives you sample language to use. It is built to help you understand your options and act on them yourself.

What Sokra is not

  • Not legal advice, and not a lawyer. Nothing here creates an attorney–client relationship.
  • Not licensed financial, tax, credit-counseling or debt-settlement advice.
  • Not a guarantee. Estimated reductions are estimates based on patterns that commonly apply; your result depends on your biller, your facts and your follow-through. Some bills can't be reduced.
  • Not a representative. Sokra never contacts anyone on your behalf.

AI limits

Sokra uses an AI model to read images and documents. It can misread a number, miss a line, or misjudge a program's rules. Check every figure against your actual bill before you rely on it, and confirm program eligibility with the organization itself. If something looks off, re-photograph the bill or ask a human professional.

Urgent situations

If you have a court date, a garnishment or levy in motion, an eviction notice, or a medical emergency, contact a professional or legal aid now. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (US). Sokra will try to flag these situations but is not a substitute for help.

Your responsibilities

  • Only upload bills that are yours or that you are authorized to handle.
  • Don't use Sokra to mislead a biller or anyone else.
  • Don't abuse the service (automated scraping, flooding, attempting to access others' plans).
