Sokra reads every line.

It checks the arithmetic, the dates, the quantities and the rules that apply to your kind of bill. Charges that appear twice. Fees added after the day they were due. A window that has not actually closed.

Then it gives you the words.

Not a summary of your situation. A script you can read down the phone tomorrow morning, with the paragraph numbers that back it up.

What it does with the page you send

Six passes over one bill, in this order.

Reads the statement

Every line, every code, every fee, including the ones printed small.

Finds what is wrong

Duplicates, arithmetic that does not add up, quantities that do not match the visit, charges added late.

Checks what the law allows

The rules for your kind of bill, in your state, and which of them your biller has to follow.

Flags the deadlines

Which windows are still open and until exactly when, including the ones nobody quotes you.

Finds the help you qualify for

Assistance and hardship programs you may already qualify for, with the income cutoffs written out.

Writes what to say

A script for the call, in order, with the citations sitting behind each line.

Paying the wrong thing first can cost you the write off.

A hospital can forgive a balance you have not paid yet. Pay it down to be responsible and the assistance you already qualified for may no longer apply to it. Settling a collections account can drop your score in the month you were about to apply for a mortgage.

That is what Sokra is built around. It tells you what to do second, not just what to do first.

What Sokra will not do with it.

Nobody is contacted for you

Sokra never calls your hospital, your bank or a collector. It hands you the words and you decide whether to use them.

Nothing is sold

No data sold or shared, no advertising trackers, and no percentage of what you save.
