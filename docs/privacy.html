Privacy

Your bill is yours.

Effective October 4, 2026. Sokra is operated by LLCreativity LLC, Tampa, Florida.

What we collect

  • The bill you upload. It is sent to an AI model (Anthropic's Claude) to be read and analyzed. By default we do not keep the file after analysis. If you tick "keep a copy," the file is stored privately so you can return to it, and you can delete it any time.
  • The analysis. We store the plan Sokra generated, the bill type, provider name, totals and estimated reductions, so your plan link works and so we can measure whether Sokra helps.
  • Context you choose to give — income, household size, state, insurance status, notes. Optional. It makes the plan more accurate.
  • Email, only if you enter it. Used to send your plan and one follow-up two weeks later asking whether it worked. Nothing else. No list, no marketing.
  • Technical data: a hashed (one-way) version of your IP address for abuse prevention, your browser type, and anonymous usage events. We don't use advertising trackers.

What we never do

  • Contact your biller, hospital, bank or collector. Sokra gives you the words; you make the call.
  • Sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
  • Use your bill to train AI models. Anthropic's API does not train on this data either.
  • Charge you to find out your options. The plan — every error, every way to reduce the bill, every script — is free and always will be. The only thing we ever charge for is Pro documents, $29, offered after you already have your free plan, and you never have to buy it.

Deleting your data

Every plan page has a "Delete my data" button. It erases the analysis, your answers, your email, any stored file, the provider's name and your hashed IP, and removes the activity log for that plan. What remains afterwards is a dated row with no content — the bill type and whether you later told us money came off — which we keep to measure whether Sokra works. If you lost the link, email sokra@llcreativityllc.com with the approximate date and provider and we'll find and remove it.

Where it lives

Data is stored on Supabase (US region) and processed by Anthropic's API (US). If you enter an email address, Resend delivers the message. If you buy Pro documents, Stripe handles the payment and we never see your card number. All are under contractual confidentiality terms.

A job runs every day that erases the contents of any plan older than 24 months — the analysis, your answers, the provider, your email and your hashed IP — leaving only an anonymous dated row. Usage events are deleted after 90 days.

Children

Sokra is for adults handling their own or their family's bills. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes and the summary will say what moved.
